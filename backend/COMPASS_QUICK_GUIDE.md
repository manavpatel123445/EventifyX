# 📸 MongoDB Compass to Atlas - Visual Guide

## **1. Download Compass:**
- Go to https://mongodb.com/try/download/compass
- Download for your OS (Windows/Mac/Linux)

## **2. Get Atlas Connection String:**
1. Go to https://cloud.mongodb.com
2. Login → Click your cluster
3. Click **"Connect"**
4. Click **"Connect your application"**
5. **Copy** the connection string

## **3. Connect in Compass:**
1. Open MongoDB Compass
2. Click **"New Connection"**
3. Paste your connection string
4. Fill in username/password/database name
5. Click **"Connect"**

## **4. Example Connection String:**
```
mongodb+srv://myuser:mypassword@cluster0.abc123.mongodb.net/EventifyX
```

## **5. If Connection Fails:**
- Check Network Access in Atlas
- Ensure cluster is running
- Verify username/password

## **6. Success:**
- See your databases in Compass
- Browse EventifyX collections
- Ready to manage your data! 🎉

**Need help? Check the detailed guide in `COMPASS_CONNECTION.md`**
